# rotd_recyclers items

The recipes use 192 item names: the items that go into the machines (materials, parts and 86 weapons) and the items that come out. An item that is not in your item list cannot be placed in a stash: a **reward** with an unknown name is skipped and the server console prints a warning every time (plus one list of all missing rewards at start), and an **input** you cannot own cannot be recycled.
